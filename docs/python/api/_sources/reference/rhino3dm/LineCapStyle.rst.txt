rhino3dm.LineCapStyle
=====================

.. py:class:: rhino3dm.LineCapStyle

   Bases: :py:obj:`enum.IntEnum`


   Shape to be used at the ends of open curves


   .. py:attribute:: Flat
      :value: 1


      Also known as butt. Ends are flat and do not extend


   .. py:attribute:: Round
      :value: 0


      Ends are extended by a half circle with diameter equal to the curve width


   .. py:attribute:: Square
      :value: 2


      Ends are flat and extended by half of the curve width
