   .. py:attribute:: DoNotShrinkNorthSide

   .. py:attribute:: DoNotShrinkSouthSide
      :value: 2


      Do not shrink on the south side of domain.


   .. py:attribute:: DoNotShrinkWestSide
      :value: 1


      Do not shrink on the west side of domain.


   .. py:attribute:: ShrinkAllSides
